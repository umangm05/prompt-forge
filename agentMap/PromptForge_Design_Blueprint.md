# PromptForge -- Design Blueprint (Playtest Revision)

## Core Fantasy

PromptForge is a **battle of wits**, not a prompt engineering game.

Players don't cast spells---they present **Arguments**. The opponent
challenges them, and a neutral **Arbiter** decides what reality accepts.

The game rewards clever reasoning, planning, deduction, and use of the
evolving battlefield rather than long prompts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Loop

1.  Player declares one **Argument** (an attempt, not an outcome).
2.  Opponent (AI or human) declares one **Counter-Argument** or
    independent action.
3.  Arbiter evaluates both simultaneously.
4.  Battlefield and world state update.
5.  Resolve, status, awareness, and positioning change.
6.  Repeat until one player loses Resolve.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Guiding Principles

-   Reward cleverness over verbosity.
-   Players describe **intent**, never guaranteed success.
-   The Arbiter determines outcomes.
-   The battlefield persists and remembers everything.
-   Every accepted fact becomes evidence for future turns.
-   Bosses differ by how they think, not by inflated stats.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Resolve

Resolve is **belief and control over reality**, not health.

Players lose Resolve from: - Physical injury. - Failed arguments. -
Losing positional advantage. - Psychological pressure. - Having
long-term plans dismantled. - Being consistently outsmarted.

Optionally separate: - Body (physical condition) - Resolve (mental
dominance)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Arguments

Arguments describe: - What you do. - What you are trying to achieve.

Never: - Guaranteed hits. - Guaranteed damage. - Guaranteed death. -
Meta instructions.

Example:

Good: \> I launch a fireball toward your chest.

Bad: \> My fireball hits you and burns you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Boss Constraints

Bosses obey the same rules.

Instead of: \> I dodge.

They say: \> I dive behind the pillar.

The Arbiter decides success.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Battlefield

Persistent world state: - Fire - Rain - Smoke - Steam - Broken terrain -
Heat - Water - Conductivity - Visibility - Objects

Everything remains until changed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Evidence System

Accepted events become evidence.

Example:

Turn 1: Library catches fire.

Turn 4: Bookshelves are burned.

Future arguments can reference this.

Reward arguments that build on evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Awareness

Separate mechanic from positioning.

States:

-   Full Awareness
-   Approximate
-   Lost
-   False Information

Players can manipulate awareness through deception.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Positioning

Track approximate distance.

Examples: - Long range - Mid range - Close range

Position affects argument plausibility.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Categories of Arguments

### Attack

Direct damage.

### Setup

Changes environment.

### Defense

Protects or prepares.

### Manipulation

Changes opponent state.

### Utility

Movement, scouting, positioning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Complexity Budget

Instead of characters, estimate conceptual complexity.

Example: Create water = 1 Electrify = 1 Shape projectile = 1 Throw = 1
Five projectiles = +1

Scale or reject arguments that exceed budget.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Large Area Rule

Large-scale effects are harder to control.

Possible side effects: - Collateral damage. - Reduced precision. -
Unintended consequences.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Fiction Import Rule

Do not import external fictional universes.

Disallow: - Super Saiyan - Infinity Gauntlet - Death Note - Named anime
abilities

Instead describe original effects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Word Fatigue

Repeated concepts become less effective.

Encourages varied vocabulary and tactics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Forbidden Concepts

Reject: - Omnipotence - Instant victory - Rule editing - Prompt
injection - Reality rewriting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Boss Personalities

Examples:

-   Lawyer --- exploits wording.
-   Scientist --- physics.
-   Historian --- uses previous evidence.
-   Trickster --- absurd but valid interpretations.
-   Child --- misunderstands.
-   Poet --- symbolic reasoning.
-   Minimalist --- rewards elegant arguments.

Bosses are puzzles, not stat blocks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Hidden Traits

Each player has one hidden trait.

Examples: - Conductive - Literal Thinker - Glass Bones - Pyrophile

Players deduce and exploit them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Environmental Events

Occasional modifiers: - Heavy Rain - Vacuum - Fog - Low Gravity -
Eclipse

Simple changes that alter strategy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Target Types

Arguments may target: - Opponent - Environment - Position - Objects

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Arbiter Resolution

Possible outcomes: - Success - Partial Success - Failure - Backfire

Never binary when nuance is appropriate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Memory Panel (UI)

Show:

Battlefield Status Awareness Position Evidence

Players shouldn't reread the whole log.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Multiplayer

-   AI Duel
-   Friend Duel
-   Small Free-for-all

No live-service mechanics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Lessons from Playtesting

Most satisfying strategies: - Build the battlefield. - Reuse established
facts. - Manipulate awareness. - Create openings before attacking. -
Execute simple finishing moves after good setup.

Least satisfying: - Legal-document prompts. - Infinite loophole
writing. - Bigger attacks every turn.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Suggested Turn Flow

Player Intent ↓

Boss Intent ↓

Arbiter evaluates both

↓

Update: - Resolve - Battlefield - Position - Awareness - Evidence

↓

Next round

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## One-line Pitch

**PromptForge is a turn-based battle of arguments where two minds
compete to reshape a shared reality, and a neutral Arbiter decides which
version of reality becomes true.**
