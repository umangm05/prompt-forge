# Boss Personalities

**Status:** built — flaws, beliefs, signature moments

## What
Each boss has goal, toolkit, tactics, **defining flaw**, biases/obsessions/pride/fear/habit/blind spots, risk appetite, characteristic temptation, and **2–4 signature moments**.
Players should recognize a boss after 1–2 turns by *how they think*, *what they use*, and *which iconic move* they threaten.

## Signatures
| Boss | Thinks like | Flaw | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lawyer | Binds rules | Cannot abandon a stamped rule | chalk, seal, cane |
| Scientist | Arena engineer | Escalates collapse even when it threatens them | plasma cutter, charge, heat glove |
| Poet | Closes tragic rings | Finishes the ring even if trapped inside | ash, banner, chant |
| Historian | Rebuilds the past | Stops to investigate new info under fire | ledger, iron spike, map |
| Trickster | Misdirects | Charges if favorite mirror dies | flare, smoke, decoy, mirror |
| Child | Obvious scare | Keeps tipping even when it falls on them | hands, rock, pry bar |
| Minimalist | One clean line | Finishes the cut when resetting would be wiser | short blade, wire, kick |

## Memorable Moments Contract
- Signature moments change relationships (terrain/control/thread), not just damage
- Readable tell → contextual execution → aftermath cost
- Emotion may override optimal play when a documented trigger hits the flaw
- Never cooldowns / mandatory phases / invented capabilities

## Conventions
- Never “I cut the railing” without a tool/method
- Escalate the plan; do not soft-reset to observation
- Adapt from **beliefs + emotion + means**, not omniscience
- Plain English, 1–2 sentences
