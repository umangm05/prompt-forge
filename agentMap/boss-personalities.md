# Boss Personalities

**Status:** built — signature goals + toolkits

## What
Each boss has goal, toolkit, tactics, and flavored sample moves.
Players should recognize a boss after 1–2 turns by *how they think* and *what they use*.

## Signatures
| Boss | Thinks like | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lawyer | Binds rules | chalk, seal, cane |
| Scientist | Arena engineer | plasma cutter, charge, heat glove |
| Poet | Closes tragic rings | ash, banner, chant |
| Historian | Rebuilds the past | ledger, iron spike, map |
| Trickster | Misdirects | flare, smoke, decoy |
| Child | Obvious scare | hands, rock, pry bar |
| Minimalist | One clean line | short blade, wire, kick |

## Conventions
- Never “I cut the railing” without a tool/method
- Escalate the plan; do not soft-reset to observation
- Plain English, 1–2 sentences
